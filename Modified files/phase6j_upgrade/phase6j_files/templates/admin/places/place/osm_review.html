Reviewer checklist · before verifying or publishing
  1. Confirm the business identity and that it is currently operating through independent evidence.
  2. Check the Kabankalan address and exact map coordinates independently, not just the OSM pin.
  3. Confirm the business category and check any possible duplicate warnings against the existing listings.
  4. Open Review / edit, record the evidence source and date, then select the verification and coordinate confirmations. Publish only when verified.
Review notes are tracked in Django Admin History when review status changes. Genuine photo rights require separate confirmation.
