Data protection

Privacy Notice

ExploreAnywhere is a place recommender for Kabankalan City. This notice explains exactly what the system stores, what it deliberately does not store, and who can see it.

Version {{ notice_version }} · {{ notice_date }}

What is stored

  • Your account: username, email address, a hashed password, the staff-assigned role and the date you accepted this notice.
  • Your activity: saved places, trip plans, reviews you wrote, visit reports you submitted, the searches you run, your relevance ratings and your usability survey answers.
  • Your feedback: everything you rate or review is attached to your account so the staff dashboard can show it next to the destination it belongs to.

What is never stored

  • Your live GPS position. The distance between you and a destination is calculated inside your browser. The coordinates are not sent to the server and are not written to the database.
  • Your precise location history. The system keeps no trail of where you have been.
  • Advertising or third-party tracking. The project sets no advertising cookies and shares nothing with advertisers.

Who can see it

You see your own saved places, plans, reviews, visits, searches and survey answers on your own pages. Staff of the city administration see the destination records, the visit reports that still need a decision, the survey answers and anonymous search statistics in the staff dashboard. Nobody else receives your data, and nothing is sold.

Maps and external services

The interactive maps load tiles from MapTiler. The Nearby map also loads its code from a public content delivery network. Tile requests reveal the map area you are looking at, in the same way as any website that shows a map. They do not receive your account, your searches or your saved places.

How long it is kept and what you can do

Your data stays on the project database until you ask for it to be removed, or until the project ends. You can ask the project administrator to delete your account; the saved places, plans, reviews, visit reports, searches and survey answers that belong to that account are removed with it. You can also ask for a copy of the data that is stored about you.

Creating an account means accepting this notice: the acceptance and its date are stored with your account. The notice is linked from the sign-up form and from the footer of every page.
